This repository contains the codes that generate the figures in "Deterministic and stochastic dynamics of a mathematical model of bistable phosphorylation and nuclear size control", by Xuesong Bai, Jonathan Touboul, and Thomas G. Fai.

ODEFUN_BistableNC2.ode is the ODE files for XPPAUT, which contains the 4D ODE system for studying the N/C ratio (Equations (10a), (10b), (10d), and (10e) in the paper).

ODEFUN_BistableCS2D1.m contains the ODE functions of the 2D ODE system for Matlab (Equations (10a) and (10b) in the paper).

Noise_FUN_BistableCS2D1.m contains the noise functions for Matlab, if the noise in the stochastic differential equations (SDEs) is multiplicative.

BistableCS2DStochasticFFT.m contains the Matlab function for solving the SDE and calculating the Fourier transform of the solution.

Figure2.m, Figure3.m, Figure4.m, Figure5.m depends on ODEFUN_BistableCS2D1.m, and generates the plots in Fig. 2, 3, 4, 5, respectively.

Figure10c_11.m depends on ODEFUN_BistableCS2D1.m and Noise_FUN_BistableCS2D1.m, and generates the plots in Fig. 10 (c) and 11.

Figure10b.m depends on BistableCS2DStochasticFFT.m, and generates the plots in Fig. 10 (b).

Figure12.m depends on ODEFUN_BistableCS2D1.m and Noise_FUN_BistableCS2D1.m, and generates the plots in Fig. 12.

Fig. 6 (a), 7, 8, 9 are generated by Matcont (codes not included).

Fig. 6 (b) and 10 (a) are generated by XPPAUT.
